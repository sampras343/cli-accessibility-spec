# HD-15: Grouped/Categorized Help

## What This Tests

Whether tools with many flags (20+) organize them under section headings or categories rather than presenting a flat list.

## How The Test Works

1. Counts flag-like lines in `--help` output
2. If fewer than 20 flags, the criterion is not applicable
3. For 20+ flags, scans for section heading patterns (title-case or uppercase lines ending with colons, or all-caps lines)
4. Counts distinct section headings

## Pass Criteria

- 3+ section headings organizing the flags
- Partially supports with 1-2 section headings

## Fail Criteria

- 20+ flags presented as a flat list without any section headings
